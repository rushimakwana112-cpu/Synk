## What's inside

| | App | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 📝 | **Blink Notes** | One beautiful note on your desktop, saved automatically and synced live with your phone. Sign in with **your own email**: your note is private. |
| ⏱ | **Screen Time** | Today's real app usage on your PC: total time and time per app, in the same colour as your note. Nothing leaves your PC. |
| 🐾 | **Blue Panther** | A blue panther cub with real feelings who lives on your taskbar. Pet him and he purrs; annoy him and he gets angry; throw him around and he cries until you make up. He chases your cursor, watches you write in Blink, reminds you of breaks and of times you write in your note, and sends you back to work when you've played too long. |
| ⚙️ | **Synk** | One window to start, stop and check everything (Start menu → **Synk**). |

## Install (Windows 10 / 11)

1. **[Download Synk-Setup.zip](https://github.com/rushimakwana112-cpu/Synk/releases/latest/download/Synk-Setup.zip)** and unzip it.
2. Double-click **`Install.cmd`**. No admin rights and nothing else needed.
3. In Blink, choose **New here? Create an account** and sign in with your email.

If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*: **More info → Run anyway**.

**Android:** install **[BlinkNotes.apk](https://github.com/rushimakwana112-cpu/Synk/releases/latest/download/BlinkNotes.apk)**
(allow "install unknown apps") and sign in with the same email.

## Always up to date

Synk updates itself: it checks for a new version when Windows starts and every few hours,
installs it and restarts by itself. The Android app asks you to update when a new version is out.

## Privacy

Every Blink account has its own private note; nobody else can read it. Screen Time and Blue Panther
keep everything on your PC.
